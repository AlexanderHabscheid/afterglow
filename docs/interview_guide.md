# Interview Guide

This is the next-step primary research artifact for validating the first feature with real users.

## Who to interview

- 5 people who have used dating apps in the last 6 months
- 2 people who recently went on an in-person first date from an app
- 1 operator, coach, or friend who often debriefs dates with others

## Core questions

1. After a first date, what do you usually do next?
2. If a dating app asked for quick feedback right after the date, what would feel too annoying?
3. What would make a post-date check-in feel useful instead of invasive?
4. Would you rather reply by SMS, in-app, or not at all?
5. What is the difference between "the date was fine" and "I want to see them again" for you?
6. How would you feel if an app nudged a second date automatically?
7. What signals would make that feel helpful versus pushy?
8. If a date felt off, what would you want the app to do with that information?

## What I want to learn

- whether users will complete a tiny check-in
- whether SMS is acceptable for an early version
- whether comfort is a more important signal than star rating alone
- whether an automated second-date suggestion feels useful or creepy

## Success criteria

- users say the flow feels low-friction
- users can understand the difference between rating and follow-up intent
- users consistently mention comfort, chemistry, or energy without being prompted
